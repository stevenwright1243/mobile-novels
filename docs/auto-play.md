# Auto

The reader's Auto button (`src/auto-play.ts`): clicking the game on by itself, at a pace learned from your own taps.

## How it works

**Auto** (the reader's top-right button, `#auto-button`, `src/auto-play.ts`; `aria-pressed`, off whenever the app starts, session-only) clicks the game on from each line by itself (`moveOn`: the text box's middle, or the page's corner in full-screen text) once you'd have read it: `waitFor(letters)` = a pause + a time per letter, learned from your own taps. Each tap of yours in the reader (`noteTap`, from portrait.ts's `tapWith` and reader taps on the background) that brings a new line (`noteText`, from `showTextLine`) notes a `ReadingSample` (the line's letters, and the time from its showing to your last tap on it), kept straight away in `settings.json`'s `readingSamples` (the last `MAX_SAMPLES`, 60; `get-`/`set-reading-samples`). `fitPace` fits them with medians (Theil–Sen: the median slope over every pair of lines, each pair weighted by how far apart its letter counts are, since equal weights let the many short lines' noise flatten the slope to ~20 ms a letter and length barely mattered; then the median pause), blended with `DEFAULT_PACE` (1.2 s + 55 ms a letter) by `DEFAULT_WEIGHT` (5 lines). Not learned from: taps with Auto on (Auto only lets you go faster than it, so they'd only teach it to speed up), lines Menus was opened on or that went away and came back (`unfair`), and times under `MIN_SAMPLE`, quicker than `SKIM_PER_LETTER` or over `MAX_SAMPLE` (skipping, or away). A reread of the same line (OCR's read corrected by memory, a line still being typed out,`SAME_LINE`) keeps its time; in full-screen text the paragraphs added since the last click count together. A click that brings no new line is tried again after `RETRY_AFTER`, `MAX_CLICKS` (3) in all; Auto rests in Menus mode (`setAutoPaused`, a fresh wait on return) and while the text is empty, unless the text box still shows writing (`setAutoBoxCheck`→`boxHasWriting()` in text-reader.ts, light boxes only): then it's a line OCR couldn't read (confirmed as '', and never re-read while the picture stays the same, which used to stall Auto for good), clicked on after a wait for the usual line's letters (`usualLetters`), each click taken to bring the next, with no retry limit; an empty box is looked at again every `LOOK_AGAIN` (1 s). While on it's accent-filled, with a bar along its bottom (`.auto-progress`) filling until the next click; its hover text gives the pace learned.
